Sudoku - Smart Hints

Brain puzzle and number game

A classic sudoku game for iPhone and iPad. When you get stuck, a Smart Hint shows you the logic behind the next step, so you learn how to solve harder puzzles instead of just being handed the answer.

Download on the App Store

Features

  • Smart Hints that explain the reasoning behind each step
  • Pencil notes for keeping track of candidates
  • Multiple difficulty levels
  • Statistics to follow your progress
  • Works offline, with no account or sign-in

Free to play

The game is free and shows an occasional ad. A one-time purchase, Remove Ads, takes the ads away for good.
